## Interpretation applied

- **Not inspected since FY2021:** The reading used is last inspection in FY2021 or earlier. The alternate is last inspection strictly before FY2021. Both readings return the same 4 foreign sites and the same 2 never-inspected sites. [E1, E2]
- **Foreign:** The reading used is an inspection-record country other than United States. Sites with no inspection record are reported as UNKNOWN country and not counted as foreign. Inferring from sponsor name was not used. [E1]
- **Currently in shortage:** Current status only, using the latest revision of each shortage record, as of 2025-10-23. [E1]
- **Manufacturing establishment:** The facility identified by FEI on the application, not the sponsor. Both the inspected firm name and sponsor name are shown. [E1]
